Visual Improvements

Transitions

Horizontal slide between steps (250ms). Forward = slide left, back = slide right. Content fades in after slide completes (150ms).

Progress Bar

Animated fill between steps (250ms easeOut). Segments show completed/current/upcoming. Teal fill with coral for current segment.

Selection Cards

Tappable option cards with teal border highlight on selection. Scale tap feedback (0.97). Selected state: teal border + subtle teal tint background.

Unit Display Fix

Unit toggle (metric/imperial) at top of Body Metrics screen. Height/weight pickers update immediately. All values format correctly for both systems.

Content Entry

Title slides up first (100ms), then subtitle fades (100ms delay), then options stagger in (50ms each). Quick but creates visual flow.

Hero Screens (1 & 9)

Keep full gradient treatment. Welcome uses teal gradient, Ready uses coral gradient. These are the "wow" moments — bookend the flow.

Merged Screen Layouts

Goal + Experience (step 3): Title "Tell us about yourself" → Goal selection cards (top section) → divider → Experience level cards (bottom section). Both single-select. One "Continue" button.

Style + Injury (step 4): Title "Your training" → Style cards (multi-select) → divider → "Any injuries or limitations?" toggle + optional detail input. One "Continue" button.

Body Metrics (step 5): Title "Your body" → Unit toggle (metric/imperial) top-right → Height picker → Weight picker. All on one screen, units update both pickers live.

Account (step 7): Title "Create your account" → Email input → Password input → Notifications permission card (with "Allow" toggle, not a separate screen).
