What Makes Each Variant Different

Standard Default card bg + border. Icon left, chevron right. For menus, lists, settings.
Workout Teal left accent bar (3px) + teal-tinted border. Status badge. Inline mini-stats. Feels active and purposeful.
Program Standard card + progress bar at bottom. Active badge in coral. Shows progression.
Stat Compact cards in a row. Icon top, large value center, label bottom. For metrics at a glance.
Action CTA Coral-tinted background + border. Stands out from other cards. Used for primary actions like "Start Workout".
Now the teal is clearly present — left accent bars, icon backgrounds, borders, status badges, progress bars, stat values. Coral only shows up for active badges and action CTAs. Does this feel right?
