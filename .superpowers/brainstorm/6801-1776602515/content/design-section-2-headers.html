Header Rules

Main tab screens (Home, Workout, Calendar, Programs, Profile)

Subtle tint background + thin bottom border. No rounded corners. No shadow. Content scrolls under it naturally.

Detail/push screens (Program Detail, Edit Profile, Settings)

Same subtle tint + back arrow. Consistent with tab screens.

Hero screens (Onboarding Welcome, Paywall)

Full gradient preserved here — these are special moments that deserve visual drama.
