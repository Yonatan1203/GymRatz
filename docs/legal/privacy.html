Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 20, 2026

GymRatz ("the App") is a fitness tracking application developed by Yonathan Glove ("Developer", "we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name provided during registration.
  • Profile Data: Profile images you upload, body measurements including height and weight.
  • Workout Data: Workout logs, exercise programs, sets, reps, weights, and achievement data you enter into the App.
  • Usage Analytics: App usage patterns, feature interactions, and crash reports collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Subscription Data: Subscription status and purchase history managed through RevenueCat.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality (tracking workouts, programs, and progress).
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • Manage your subscription and provide access to premium features.
  • Analyze app usage to improve performance and user experience.
  • Diagnose technical issues and fix bugs.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud Platform). All data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest on Google's servers. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect or process your data:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Analytics, and Crashlytics. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and in-app purchase management. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently purged from our systems.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can view all your data within the App at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account from within the App, which permanently removes all your data.
  • Export: You may contact us to request an export of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the updated policy will be posted on this page and within the App. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
