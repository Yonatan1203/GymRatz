Delete Your Account

You can request the deletion of your GymRatz account and all associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the GymRatz app on your device
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing us at gymratzapp@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account" and the email address associated with your account.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and profile image
  • Workout data: All workout logs, exercise records, sets, reps, and weights
  • Programs: Any workout programs you created
  • Progress data: Personal records, body measurements, and achievement history
  • Coach relationships: Any coach-trainee connections and assigned programs

What Data May Be Retained

  • Subscription records: Purchase history may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat for billing and refund purposes, in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
  • Analytics data: Anonymized, aggregated usage data that cannot be linked back to you may be retained.
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through Google Play before deleting your account to avoid future charges.

Processing Time

In-app account deletion is processed immediately. Email requests are processed within 7 business days.
